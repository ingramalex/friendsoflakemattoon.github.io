What this measures — and what it doesn't

Lake Paradise is Mattoon's primary drinking water source. Lake Mattoon is a contingency source. The July 2025 “Do Not Drink” order followed a bloom in Lake Paradise; per the City's own recovery report, no drinking water was drawn from Lake Mattoon during those events. These readings are of finished, treated tap water leaving the plant — not of the lake itself.

Microcystin is a cyanotoxin produced by blue-green algae and is primarily a liver toxin. The City's notices describe it as a “neurological toxin,” which is imprecise. The 0.30 ppb line is the EPA advisory for sensitive groups — children under 6, pregnant and nursing women, people with liver conditions, dialysis patients, the elderly, and immunocompromised people. For healthy adults the advisory is 1.60 ppb.

How it works

A scheduled job runs every Monday morning. It has two halves, kept deliberately separate.

Automatic

Collecting readings

A plain script re-reads the City's cumulative water quality notice and rebuilds the full series from scratch. No AI is involved, so a correction on the City's side flows straight through and there is nothing to second-guess.

Reviewed

Finding new sources

Claude searches for government and institutional sources we don't already track, then files them for review. Nothing an AI proposes is published automatically — a bad source on this page would undermine the exact credibility it exists to build.
