## Water quality

The most recent reading, taken **Nov. 16, 2026, was 0.0 ppb** — unchanged from the Oct. 20 reading, also 0.0. Source: the City's [water quality update of Nov. 19](https://mattoon.illinois.gov/public-notices/water-quality-update-november-19-2026/).

The run since our last brief:

| Date | ppb |
|---|---|
| Aug. 10 | 0.009 |
| Aug. 24 | 0.053 |
| Sept. 7 | 0.0 |
| Sept. 21 | 0.06 |
| Oct. 5 | 0.21 |
| Oct. 20 | 0.0 |
| Nov. 16 | 0.0 |

The two advisory levels in use are 0.3 ppb for sensitive groups and 1.6 ppb for the general population. The October 5 reading of 0.21 is the highest in the entire 82-reading record and still sat under the lower of the two. There have been no exceedances across those 82 readings.

Two things worth keeping straight. These are **finished treated tap water** samples — they describe what comes out of a city tap, not what's in the water at your dock. And Lake Paradise is the City's primary drinking water source; Lake Mattoon is the contingency source. The July 2025 Do Not Drink order followed a bloom in Lake Paradise.

## Watershed planning

No new activity to report. The Coles County SWCD's [Lake Mattoon and Lake Paradise Watershed Committee page](http://www.colescountyswcd.org/resources/watersheds/lake-mattoon-lake-paradise-watershed-committee/) was last modified July 1, 2026, and still advertises a July 8 meeting at the Mattoon Water Treatment Plant as "next." That July agenda floated setting a standing schedule — first Wednesday of every odd month — but no later meeting date has been posted. Membership is open to anyone willing to help, whether or not you live in the watershed; the contact is mattoonandparadisewatershed@gmail.com.

Nothing new this week on TMDL work, watershed funding, or shoreline erosion.

Elsewhere: a September City [tourism grant announcement](https://mattoon.illinois.gov/city-news/city-of-mattoon-selected-to-receive-tourism-marketing-partnership-grant/) lists Lake Mattoon among local attractions, with no detail beyond the mention. Recent Shelby County, Cumberland County, and Neoga agendas were searched and contained no lake items.
