## Water quality

The City's [latest water quality update](https://mattoon.illinois.gov/public-notices/water-quality-update-july-30-2026/), dated July 30, shows a microcystin reading of **0.071 ppb on July 26**. Nothing newer has been posted, so the most recent number is now more than two weeks old.

Recent readings: 0.039 (June 15), 0.030 (June 30), 0.069 (July 11), 0.098 (July 12), 0.104 (July 13), 0.071 (July 26). The advisory threshold for sensitive groups is 0.3 ppb; the general-population threshold is 1.6 ppb. Across 75 readings the highest was 0.14 ppb and there have been no exceedances.

Two things to keep straight. These are finished, treated tap water samples from the city system — they are not measurements of lake water at your shoreline or your dock. And Lake Paradise is the City's primary drinking water source; Lake Mattoon is a contingency source. The July 2025 Do Not Drink order followed a bloom in Lake Paradise, not here. Microcystin is a liver toxin.

## Watershed planning

The [Lake Mattoon and Lake Paradise Watershed Committee](http://www.colescountyswcd.org/resources/watersheds/lake-mattoon-lake-paradise-watershed-committee/) is a new group formed with the City of Mattoon. Its page still advertises a July 8 meeting at the Mattoon Water Treatment Plant, and was last modified July 1 — so the only posted date has passed with no replacement announced and no minutes published.

The [July 8 agenda](http://www.colescountyswcd.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2026.7.8-MP-Watershed-Meeting-Agenda.docx) shows what the group planned to take up: an overview of what a watershed committee does, the watershed's history, short- and long-term goals, setting the next meeting date, and a proposal to meet the first Wednesday of every odd month. Whether that schedule was adopted is unknown. Membership is open to anyone willing to help, whether or not you live in the watershed; email mattoonandparadisewatershed@gmail.com for updates.

No TMDL, funding, or erosion news surfaced in this week's evidence.
