## Water quality

The City's latest posted result for microcystin — a liver toxin — in **finished, treated tap water** is **0.0 ppb on Oct. 20** ([water quality update, Oct. 23, 2026](https://mattoon.illinois.gov/public-notices/water-quality-update-october-23-2026/)).

Readings since our last brief:

- Aug. 10 — 0.009 ppb
- Aug. 24 — 0.053 ppb
- Sept. 7 — 0.0 ppb
- Sept. 21 — 0.06 ppb
- Oct. 5 — 0.21 ppb
- Oct. 20 — 0.0 ppb

The Oct. 5 result of 0.21 ppb is the highest in the entire 81-reading series. The advisory threshold for sensitive groups is 0.3 ppb; the general-population threshold is 1.6 ppb. There have been no exceedances across the 81 readings.

Two things to keep straight. First, these are treated tap-water numbers, not lake-surface numbers. Second, Lake Paradise is the City's primary drinking water source and Lake Mattoon is a contingency source; the July 2025 Do Not Drink order followed a bloom in Lake Paradise.

The gap worth noting is the calendar: the most recent posted reading is nearly four weeks old. We have no evidence of a reading after Oct. 20, and no evidence that sampling stopped. We just don't know.

## Watershed planning

Nothing new. The Coles County SWCD page for the [Lake Mattoon and Lake Paradise Watershed Committee](http://www.colescountyswcd.org/resources/watersheds/lake-mattoon-lake-paradise-watershed-committee/) has not been updated since July 1, and the meeting it still advertises was July 8 at the Mattoon Water Treatment Plant. That July agenda proposed setting a recurring schedule — possibly the first Wednesday of every odd month — but no later meeting date has been posted. The committee is open to anyone, in the watershed or not; email **mattoonandparadisewatershed@gmail.com** to get on the update list.

No TMDL news and no erosion-project news appeared in this week's evidence.

The only other planning-adjacent mention was a [City tourism marketing grant announcement](https://mattoon.illinois.gov/city-news/city-of-mattoon-selected-to-receive-tourism-marketing-partnership-grant/) that lists Lake Mattoon among local attractions.
