## Water quality

The latest sample, collected **September 7, 2026**, came back at **0.0 ppb** — the first flat zero in the recent run. The three readings new since our last brief: August 10 at 0.009 ppb, August 24 at 0.053 ppb, September 7 at 0.0 ppb. Before those: July 26 at 0.071, July 13 at 0.104, July 12 at 0.098.

For context, the recommended advisory level for sensitive groups is 0.30 ppb and 1.6 ppb for the general population. Across all 78 samples in the record, the highest single reading is 0.14 ppb and there have been no exceedances. These are finished treated tap water, not lake water. Lake Paradise is the City's primary drinking water source; Lake Mattoon is a contingency source.

One wording note: the City's [September 11 update](https://mattoon.illinois.gov/public-notices/water-quality-update-september-11-2026/) describes the substance as a "harmful algal bloom neurological toxin." Microcystin is a liver toxin, not a neurological one. The same notices describe a reported number such as 0.053 ppb as "non-detectable levels."
