## Why this matters if you live on the lake

Most Lake Mattoon residents live outside Mattoon city limits and do not vote for this council. That makes showing up and asking questions the main available lever.

Lake Paradise is the City's primary drinking water source; Lake Mattoon is a contingency source. Both were named in the watershed discussion, so decisions made in this two-year process can reach Lake Mattoon regardless of where the City draws water day to day.

## Questions worth asking

- Who is the consultant, what is the contract worth, and what is the data-collection scope in year one?
- Will the watershed process include a way for non-resident lakefront property owners to give input?
- Does the hydraulic modeling study's dredging component touch Lake Mattoon, or only Lake Paradise and City facilities?
- When will the RFP responses come back to council?
