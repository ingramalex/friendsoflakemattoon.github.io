## Why lake residents may want to follow this

Standing background, not from this meeting: Lake Paradise is the City of Mattoon's primary drinking water source, and Lake Mattoon serves as a contingency source. Treatment decisions at the plant sit downstream of whichever source is in use, so changes in treatment technology are part of the same system that touches Lake Mattoon when the contingency source is drawn on. A six-month trial means there will be a results phase — that is the point at which specifics are most likely to become public.
