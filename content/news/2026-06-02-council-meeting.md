Lake Paradise is the City's primary drinking water source and Lake Mattoon is a contingency source, so a multi-year water supply plan is likely to touch both — but the recording, as summarized, does not say so.

## Marina boat slips

The council took up Special Ordinance No. 2026-2011, assigning ownership for boat slip construction and rentals at the marina from one G&H Services entity to another — reportedly LLC-2 to LLC-3 ([03:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YLhOZMw2MiI&t=206s)). Staff described this as an internal transfer between entities that does not change who actually owns or operates the marina facilities.

The ordinance passed 5-0 ([04:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YLhOZMw2MiI&t=274s)).
