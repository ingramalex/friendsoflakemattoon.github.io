## Water quality

The most recent finished tap water sample, taken **August 24, was 0.053 ppb**. The August 10 sample was 0.009 ppb. Both are below the 0.30 ppb advisory level for sensitive groups and well below the 1.6 ppb general advisory. For context, July's samples ran 0.069, 0.098, 0.104 and 0.071 ppb. Across 77 samples the highest reading has been 0.14 ppb, with no exceedances. The reading went up between the two August samples; it remains far under the advisory thresholds. Source: the City's [Water Quality Update dated August 28](https://mattoon.illinois.gov/public-notices/water-quality-update-august-28-2026/).

Two notes on that notice's wording. It describes each sample as showing "non-detectable levels" while listing a number above zero — 0.104 ppb on July 13, for instance. And it calls the substance a "neurological toxin." Microcystin is a liver toxin, not a neurological one. We flag this once and will not repeat it each week.

These samples are treated tap water. Lake Paradise is the City's primary drinking water source; Lake Mattoon is a contingency source. The July 2025 Do Not Drink order followed a bloom in Lake Paradise.
