# Week of Nov. 9: No Council Meeting Posted for the Rest of November, and Tap Readings Back at Zero

## Coming up

The City of Mattoon's [upcoming meetings page](https://mattoon.illinois.gov/government/citycouncil/upcomingmeetings/) currently lists **no scheduled meetings**. The last two on record are **Nov. 4** (posted as rescheduled) and **Oct. 20**. The page states the council's cadence as the 1st and 3rd Tuesdays, which would point to Tuesday, Nov. 17 — but nothing for that date is posted yet, so treat it as unconfirmed and check the page before driving in.

Agendas and packets are behind a CAPTCHA, so only the meeting dates and document titles could be read. **Whether anything touching Lake Mattoon is on an upcoming agenda is unknown this week** — not absent, unreadable.

Most of us who live on the lake are outside Mattoon city limits and do not vote for this council, which nonetheless governs the lake. Public comment at a council meeting is still open to you.

The **Lake Mattoon and Lake Paradise Watershed Committee** has not posted a new meeting. Its [page](http://www.colescountyswcd.org/resources/watersheds/lake-mattoon-lake-paradise-watershed-committee/) was last modified July 1 and still advertises the July 8 meeting at the Mattoon Water Treatment Plant. The July agenda included setting a recurring schedule — possibly first Wednesdays of odd months — but no result has been posted. The committee is open to anyone, in the watershed or not: mattoonandparadisewatershed@gmail.com.

## Water quality

The most recent result in the City's [water quality update](https://mattoon.illinois.gov/public-notices/water-quality-update-october-23-2026/) is from **Oct. 20: 0.0 ppb**. Six results have posted since our last brief:

- Aug. 10 — 0.009
- Aug. 24 — 0.053
- Sept. 7 — 0.0
- Sept. 21 — 0.06
- Oct. 5 — 0.21
- Oct. 20 — 0.0

The City reports against two advisory levels: 0.3 ppb for sensitive groups and 1.6 ppb for the general population. The Oct. 5 result, 0.21, is the highest in the full record of 81 samples and sits below both. There have been no exceedances.

Two things worth keeping straight. These are **finished treated tap water** samples, not lake water — they describe what comes out of the tap, not conditions at a dock or a boat ramp. And Lake Paradise is the City's primary source; Lake Mattoon is a contingency source. No newer result has posted in the nineteen days since Oct. 20.
