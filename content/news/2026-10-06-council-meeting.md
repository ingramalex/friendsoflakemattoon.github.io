## What the council committed to

The council voted to award a contract for repairs to the Lake Mattoon Dam. Council Decision Request 2026-2732 awards a bid of $74,200 to a contractor the captions render as Fuller-Wente, Inc., and authorizes the mayor to sign an IDOT Local Public Agency formal contract for the work.

The vote passed unanimously, 5-0 ([18:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_2ux8VDItcI&t=1098s)).

## What the work involves

In discussion before the vote, the commissioner identified in the captions as Clawson described the project as patching the spillway flume ([17:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_2ux8VDItcI&t=1045s)). Per that discussion, the work could not begin until lake water levels dropped below the spillway crest — meaning the timing of the repair depends on lake conditions, not just the contract date.

The bid came in below the city's cost estimate, according to the same discussion. The findings do not include a start date, a completion date, or any description of how the work might affect lake access or water levels. Anyone planning around the spillway area should treat the schedule as unannounced.
