## Water quality

The [city's Oct. 23 water quality update](https://mattoon.illinois.gov/public-notices/water-quality-update-october-23-2026/) reports microcystin in finished treated tap water at 0.0 ppb on Oct. 20. Readings since our last brief:

- Aug. 10 — 0.009 ppb
- Aug. 24 — 0.053 ppb
- Sept. 7 — 0.0 ppb
- Sept. 21 — 0.06 ppb
- Oct. 5 — 0.21 ppb
- Oct. 20 — 0.0 ppb

The Oct. 5 figure is the highest in the series of 81 samples. The advisory threshold for sensitive groups is 0.3 ppb and the general threshold is 1.6 ppb; there have been no exceedances of either. Microcystin is a liver toxin.

Two things worth keeping straight: these are treated tap numbers, not lake-surface numbers, and they do not describe the water at your dock. Lake Paradise is the city's primary drinking water source and Lake Mattoon is a contingency source.
