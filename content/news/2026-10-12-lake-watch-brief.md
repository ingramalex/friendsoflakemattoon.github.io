# Oct 12: No Council agenda posted yet for the 20th, and the watershed committee hasn't posted a meeting since July

## Coming up

The Mattoon City Council meets on the **1st and 3rd Tuesdays**. Its [most recent meeting was Oct 6](https://mattoon.illinois.gov/download/city-council-meeting-2026-10-06/), and nothing is yet listed on the [upcoming meetings page](https://mattoon.illinois.gov/government/citycouncil/upcomingmeetings/). On that cadence the next regular meeting would fall on Tuesday, Oct 20, but we can't confirm a date or an agenda item until the city posts one.

We also can't tell you what's on any agenda. The agenda and packet PDFs sit behind a CAPTCHA, so only meeting dates and document titles are readable from outside. If a lake item is scheduled, we won't know until someone opens the packet by hand or watches the meeting.

Worth remembering: most people who live on Lake Mattoon are outside Mattoon city limits and don't vote for the council that governs the lake. The meetings are still open, and they're [posted to YouTube afterward](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_2ux8VDItcI).

The **Lake Mattoon and Lake Paradise Watershed Committee** is open to anyone, in the watershed or not. Its [page](http://www.colescountyswcd.org/resources/watersheds/lake-mattoon-lake-paradise-watershed-committee/) was last updated July 1 and still advertises a July 8 meeting at the Mattoon Water Treatment Plant — three months past. That July agenda included setting a next date and raised the question of a standing schedule (first Wednesday of odd months was floated as a question, not a decision). No later meeting has been posted. To get email updates directly: mattoonandparadisewatershed@gmail.com.

Of six recent documents checked each, the Shelby County, Cumberland County, and Neoga boards had no lake items.

## Water quality

The most recent published reading is **Sept 21: 0.06 ppb** in finished treated tap water. The run since midsummer: 0.104 (Jul 13), 0.071 (Jul 26), 0.009 (Aug 10), 0.053 (Aug 24), 0.00 (Sep 7), 0.06 (Sep 21). The advisory level for sensitive groups is 0.3 ppb; the general-population level is 1.6 ppb. Across 79 samples the highest result has been 0.14 ppb, with no exceedances. Nothing has changed in the direction or scale of these numbers. The [Sept 23 update](https://mattoon.illinois.gov/public-notices/water-quality-update-september-23-2026/) is the newest notice; the latest reading in it is now three weeks old.

Two points of context. These are tap-water results for the City of Mattoon's system, where **Lake Paradise is the primary source and Lake Mattoon is a contingency source**; the July 2025 Do Not Drink order followed a bloom in Lake Paradise. And the notices describe the result as a "harmful algal bloom neurological toxin." The toxin measured against these 0.3/1.6 ppb advisory levels is microcystin, which is a **liver** toxin, not a neurological one. We'll note that once and not repeat it.
