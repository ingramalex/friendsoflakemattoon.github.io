## Water quality

The [City's October 23 water quality update](https://mattoon.illinois.gov/public-notices/water-quality-update-october-23-2026/) reports **0.0 ppb microcystin** in finished treated tap water on October 20. Six results have posted since our last brief:

- Aug 10 — 0.009 ppb
- Aug 24 — 0.053 ppb
- Sep 7 — 0.0 ppb
- Sep 21 — 0.06 ppb
- Oct 5 — 0.21 ppb
- Oct 20 — 0.0 ppb

The October 5 reading of 0.21 ppb is the highest in the whole 81-result record. It is below the 0.3 ppb advisory level for sensitive groups and well below the 1.6 ppb general advisory, and the next sample came back at zero. There have been no exceedances across all 81 readings.

Two things to keep straight. These are treated tap water numbers, not lake numbers. And the City's primary drinking water source is Lake Paradise; Lake Mattoon is a contingency source. Microcystin is a liver toxin.
