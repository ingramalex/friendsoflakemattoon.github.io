# Week of September 28: Watershed Committee Has Posted Nothing Since July; Tap Readings Stay Low

## Coming up

No future Mattoon City Council dates were posted on the city's [upcoming meetings page](https://mattoon.illinois.gov/government/citycouncil/upcomingmeetings/) when it was checked. The council's stated cadence is the 1st and 3rd Tuesdays, which would put the next regular meeting on October 6, but that is arithmetic from the cadence, not a posted date — confirm before you drive in. The most recent posted meetings were [September 15](https://mattoon.illinois.gov/download/city-council-meeting-2026-09-15/) and [September 1](https://mattoon.illinois.gov/download/city-council-meeting-2026-09-01/). We could not tell you whether anything on those agendas touches the lake: the agenda and packet PDFs sit behind a CAPTCHA, so only the meeting dates and document titles were readable.

Worth repeating for the many of us who live on the lake but outside Mattoon city limits: this council governs the lake, and most lakefront households have no vote in it. Public comment is the channel that is open to everyone.

The [Lake Mattoon and Lake Paradise Watershed Committee](http://www.colescountyswcd.org/resources/watersheds/lake-mattoon-lake-paradise-watershed-committee/) page still advertises a July 8 meeting at the Mattoon Water Treatment Plant; the page was last modified July 1 and no meeting since has been posted. That July agenda included a question about whether to adopt a standing schedule — first Wednesday of every odd month was the suggestion — but nothing on the page says whether the group adopted it. The committee is open to anyone, in the watershed or not; email mattoonandparadisewatershed@gmail.com to get on their list.

No TMDL, funding, or erosion news appeared in this week's evidence.

## Water quality

Four new results since the last brief, all from finished treated tap water:

| Date | ppb |
|---|---|
| Aug 10 | 0.009 |
| Aug 24 | 0.053 |
| Sep 7 | 0.00 |
| Sep 21 | 0.06 |

The sensitive-population advisory level is 0.30 ppb and the general advisory level is 1.6 ppb. The latest reading, 0.06 ppb, is the highest since July but remains well under both. Across 79 samples the highest recorded value is 0.14 ppb and there have been no exceedances. Source: the city's [September 23 water quality update](https://mattoon.illinois.gov/public-notices/water-quality-update-september-23-2026/).

One wording note, made once: the city's notices describe these results as a "harmful algal bloom neurological toxin." Microcystin is a liver toxin, not a neurological one. The numbers are the numbers; the label in the notice is wrong.

Context that gets muddled locally: Lake Paradise is Mattoon's primary drinking water source and Lake Mattoon is a contingency source. The July 2025 Do Not Drink order followed a bloom in Lake Paradise.
