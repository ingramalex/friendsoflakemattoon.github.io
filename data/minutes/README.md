# Council documents added by hand

The City of Mattoon puts its council agendas, packets, and minutes behind a
CAPTCHA. That is the City's signal against automated downloading, and this
project does not work around it. A CAPTCHA exists to tell people apart from
bots — a person clicking through one is its intended use, not a loophole.

So the download stays manual and everything after it is automatic.

## How to add a document

1. Open the meeting page, e.g.
   https://mattoon.illinois.gov/government/citycouncil/upcomingmeetings/
2. Click through to the meeting and download the Agenda, Packet, or Minutes.
3. Save the PDF here, named with the meeting date first:

       2026-08-04-council-minutes.pdf
       2026-08-04-council-agenda.pdf
       2026-08-18-council-packet.pdf

4. Commit it. The next brief reads it automatically, pulls out anything about
   the lake or the watershed, and cites it.

Files whose text cannot be extracted (scans rather than text PDFs) are
reported as unreadable rather than silently skipped.

## Why this matters

This is the only path by which the *contents* of a council agenda reach the
brief. Without it, the brief can say a meeting happened and what documents
were filed, but not what was discussed — which is the part that actually
tells you whether to show up.
