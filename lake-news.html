How this is put together — and where it stops

Meeting dates, document titles, and every number are scraped directly from government sources. The write-up is drafted by AI from that evidence only, then reviewed by a person before it is published. Nothing posts itself.

Two limits worth knowing. Mattoon City Council agenda and packet PDFs are behind a CAPTCHA, so we can see that a meeting exists and what documents were filed, but not what is on the agenda. And where a source can't be reached, the brief says so rather than implying nothing happened.

Sources: City of Mattoon public notices and council meeting listings; Coles County Soil & Water Conservation District; Coles, Shelby, and Cumberland county boards; Illinois EPA.
